# THE FLOOR
A financial newspaper collided with a noisy trading pit.

**Twelve egos. One balance sheet.**

The name stays THE FLOOR. Describe the show as an AI trading-floor experiment or fictional proprietary trading house. Do not imply it is a licensed broker or that a token is company equity.

## Visual system
- Paper #F4EFE4: page, receipts, editorial space.
- Ink #191923: type, public boards, heavy rules.
- Cobalt #294CFF: identity, navigation, calls to action.
- Orange #FF663D: interruptions, principal reactions, accents.
- Gains and losses retain distinct green/red status colors; green is no longer the brand background.
- Barlow Condensed for tall trading-pit headlines, DM Sans for readable prose, IBM Plex Mono for figures.
- Square panels, strong dividing lines, offset print-like shadows. No decorative gradients or rounded dashboard cards.
- The stepped F mark is the favicon and header identity. A reusable wordmark lives in public/brand/wordmark.svg. The social image is generated at /opengraph-image.

## Voice
Short, opinionated, visibly human: “Big egos. Small margins.” “The boss is listening.” “Every fill has a receipt.”
Use the joke for the characters; use precise labels for money. Paper treasury, token market cap, wallet SOL, and unclaimed fees always remain distinct.

## Models
Original faceted characters, oversized eyes, chunky shoes, cobalt ties, contrasting desk jackets. Straight standing legs and separately jointed knees support actual walking and seated poses. The office uses stone, dark ink, muted blue furnishings, and warm desk wood.
